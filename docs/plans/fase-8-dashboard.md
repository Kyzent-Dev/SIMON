## Exact values
- **Triwulan** — `App\Services\Triwulan` (reads `rekap.dasar_triwulan` once per instance):
  - `tanggalDasar(Spt $spt): ?CarbonImmutable` = the SPT's `tanggal_selesai` / `tanggal_mulai` / `tanggal_lhp` per the setting; when the setting is `tanggal_lhp` and `tanggal_lhp` is null, fall back to `tanggal_selesai` (record it). Null when the chosen date is null.
  - `dari(DateTimeInterface $d): int` = 1–4 by month (Jan–Mar = 1 …). `rentang(int $tahun, int $tw): array{CarbonImmutable, CarbonImmutable}` first/last day.
  - For PKPT items the basis date is the effective range (`KesesuaianPkpt::rentang()`, approved revision first): its end date, or its start date when the setting is `tanggal_mulai` (record it).
  - An SPT or item belongs to year Y and TW n when its basis date falls in that TW of year Y (the `spt.tahun` column is not used for rekap; it is used for the Tahun filter of monitoring and dashboards — record both).
- **Rekap Triwulan** — `App\Services\RekapTriwulan::hitung(int $tahun, ?int $irbanId = null): array` (null = all irban). Counts:
  - SPT in scope: `status_pengajuan = diterima` (Dibatalkan, Ditolak, drafts and pending excluded), grouped by the TW of `Triwulan::tanggalDasar()`; SPT without a basis date are excluded.
  - **Rencana** (per irban per TW): sum of `pkpt_item.target_spt` (null counts as 1 — one item plans at least one SPT; record it) of the items of the year's PKPT with status **Disahkan** (no Disahkan PKPT → 0 and the page shows the alert `PKPT {tahun} belum disahkan; rencana belum dapat dihitung.`), TW by the item basis date above.
  - **Realisasi** (per irban per TW): SPT in scope with `pkpt_item_id` not null and status Selesai.
  - `persen` = `round(realisasi / rencana * 100)` when rencana > 0, else null (shown as `–`).
  - **Per Klasifikasi** (per TW and total): SPT in scope per klasifikasi: `jumlah` (all in scope), `selesai`, `berjalan` (On Process).
  - **Per Jenis Penugasan** (per TW and total): rencana (sum `target_spt` of items of that jenis, as above) and realisasi (Selesai SPT of that jenis with an item), plus `jumlah` (all SPT in scope of that jenis, incl. non-PKPT).
  - Totals row per table (sum over the rows; `persen` recomputed from the totals, never averaged).
  - Return shape is plain arrays (no models), documented in the docblock, used by the page, the xlsx export and the PDF alike.
- **Monitoring** — `App\Services\MonitoringSpt`:
  - `baris(array $filter): array` returns groups: one per PKPT item (ordered by item effective start, then id) with the item's jenis, effective period, irban and its SPT rows; then the group `Di luar PKPT` (SPT without item). Each SPT row: id, nomor_lengkap, irban, jenis penugasan, realisasi period, kesesuaian (`sesuai` / `tidak_sesuai` / `non_pkpt`), status (`StatusSpt`), flags (from `PenandaSpt`), kendala (kategori + uraian or null), url.
  - Rows in scope: `status_pengajuan = diterima` (monitoring is about realization). Filters (all optional, validated): `tahun` (spt.tahun, default current year), `tw` (1–4 via `Triwulan`), `irban` (id), `status` (`berjalan` = On Process, `selesai`, or a single On Process `StatusSpt` value), `jenis` (jenis_penugasan id), `kesesuaian` (`sesuai` / `tidak_sesuai` / `non_pkpt`), `penanda` (`terlambat`), `penjelasan` (`terbuka` = SPT with an open Minta Penjelasan, `Komentar::permintaanTerbuka()`).
